## Start with a thought

Describe a subject, the light and the feeling. Choose a supported image format and a creative direction, or keep the words exactly as you wrote them. Lumina creates one image per request.

## Keep the good things

Your results and prompts stay in a private library. Open a creation to download the original output, reuse the prompt or remove a finished record. Deleting an image does not refund used credits.

## Follow a clear state

Queued, creating, ready and uncertain mean different things. If a connection is interrupted, recover the same request instead of submitting another one. Reopen a creation to resume status checks; Lumina does not run an unattended generation queue.

This is a source-code example. Real model generation and paid checkout are disabled until the workspace operator configures them. Local fixture artwork is labelled and is not AI-generated.
