## Sample policy, not a production notice

This is original example content for the SaaSStarterKit starter. The site owner must replace it with an accurate privacy notice identifying the operating entity, contact details, applicable rights, retention periods, and the services actually enabled before a public launch.

## Account information

The application stores the account details you provide, including your name, email address, profile image, and authentication records. Session information helps maintain signed-in access. Password-based authentication is managed by Better Auth; passwords should never be logged or displayed.

## Files and optional services

Uploaded files are stored in a private object store and associated with your account. Optional payment, email, newsletter, and AI providers may receive the information required for the operation you request. The operating site must name its actual providers and explain the relevant data transfers.

## Your controls

You can update your profile, revoke sessions, delete API keys, unsubscribe from optional newsletters, and request account deletion in settings. The operator must explain any legally required retention and the process for exercising applicable rights.

## Contact

Use the contact page to reach the site operator once its support destination has been configured. Do not send sensitive information in an ordinary support form.
