## Example notice

This notice is a starter example. The operating site must audit the cookies and storage it actually uses, identify their lifetimes and providers, and implement any consent controls required for its audience.

## Essential authentication

The application uses authentication cookies to maintain a signed-in session. These cookies should use secure settings appropriate to the deployment and should not be exposed to client-side scripts when an HTTP-only cookie is required.

## Preferences

The color-theme preference is saved in your browser’s local storage. The language is represented in the URL: English uses an unprefixed path and Chinese uses /zh. Clearing site storage can reset saved preferences.

## Optional integrations

Analytics and chat integrations are optional and disabled in the starter’s default configuration. Enabling a third-party service may add cookies, browser storage, or network requests. The operator must update this notice and any required consent mechanism before enabling them.
