## Sample terms requiring review

These are original starter examples, not an executed agreement or legal advice. Before public use, the operator must define the contracting entity, service, eligibility, acceptable use, pricing, refunds, jurisdiction, and other terms appropriate to the actual product.

## Using the application

Keep your account credentials private and provide accurate account information. Do not attempt to access another person’s resources, disrupt the service, or use the product for unlawful purposes. Review generated AI content before relying on it.

## Optional paid services

The pricing page contains an example catalog. A configured payment provider presents the actual checkout, taxes, billing period, and payment terms. A completed redirect alone does not grant paid access; the application waits for verified provider confirmation.

## Cancellation and deletion

Subscription cancellation, where supported, takes effect at the end of the paid period. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund. Account deletion is a separate action. The operator must publish its applicable refund and retention rules.

## Availability

Optional integrations depend on their configuration and third-party availability. This starter does not promise uninterrupted service or production readiness without the operator’s own configuration, review, and validation.
